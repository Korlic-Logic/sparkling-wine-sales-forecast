# Sparkling Wine Sales Forecast

Time-series forecasting project for ABC Estate Wines, predicting monthly sparkling wine sales.

## Business Context

ABC Estate Wines sells different types of wine, and sales data from the 20th century is available for analysis. As an analyst, the task is to analyze historical sparkling wine sales and build a model to forecast future sales, helping the company plan production and inventory.

## Objective

Analyze historical sales data and build time-series forecasting models (e.g. ARIMA/SARIMA) to predict future sparkling wine sales.
